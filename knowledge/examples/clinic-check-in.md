## Arrival

The fictional Riverbend test clinic asks visitors to confirm their appointment time at the front desk. Staff can direct anyone who needs help to the reception desk.

## Changes to an appointment

For this demonstration, visitors who need to change an appointment should contact the fictional clinic directly. The example intentionally contains no real contact details or medical advice.
