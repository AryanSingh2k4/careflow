# Documents and test requisitions

**Fictional demo content:** This page describes an invented CareFlow Demo Clinic portfolio. It is not a document submission portal and does not provide clinical or legal advice.

If a clinician issued a referral, requisition, or other paperwork, retain the original instructions and ask the issuing provider how it should be presented. The required documents can depend on the provider and the purpose of the visit. This portfolio cannot verify a document, sign a form, renew a requisition, or confirm that a particular service accepts it. No uploads or patient records are available here. Avoid placing personal identifiers or document images in a general chat.

For requested tests, follow preparation instructions supplied by the issuing team. This page does not specify fasting or medication changes. If preparation details are missing, contact that team using its verified channel. Consultation and test fees are separate; ask the provider for a separate test quote and availability. A requisition alone does not reserve an appointment or confirm that the service is available.

For pediatric appointments, a responsible adult should check directly with the real clinic about forms, consent, and identity verification. Do not assume this fictional example’s process satisfies a real provider’s rules. Reception in the demo operates Monday–Saturday, 09:00–17:00 IST, excluding Sundays and posted holidays; these hours do not guarantee a clinician or test slot. This administrative page cannot assess the medical content of any document.
