# Dr. James Wilson — Cardiology

**Fictional demo content:** CareFlow Demo Clinic and this clinician profile are invented for a software portfolio. Nothing here assesses symptoms or replaces advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

Dr. James Wilson is the fictional cardiology clinician represented in this portfolio. The initial cardiology consultation fee shown for the demo is ₹900. A follow-up costs ₹300 only when a clinician has requested it for the same specialty and same concern, and it takes place within 14 calendar days. In other circumstances, the initial fee applies. Tests, if any are requested, have a separate quote. This profile does not promise that the clinic conducts a particular test or operates a laboratory.

The profile is not a medical biography and makes no claims about credentials, treatment outcomes, appointment times, or availability. Appointment options are shown by the live booking calendar. Reception hours are Monday through Saturday, 09:00–17:00 IST, with Sundays and posted holidays closed; these hours are not a promise that cardiology slots are available then. Patients should plan to arrive 15 minutes early. Arriving more than 10 minutes late may mean rebooking.

For a changed plan, the fictional clinic suggests 12 hours’ notice if possible and charges no cancellation or no-show fee. Avoid posting personal health details in a general chat. For clinical advice, contact an appropriate healthcare professional through a suitable care channel. This page is for administrative orientation only.
