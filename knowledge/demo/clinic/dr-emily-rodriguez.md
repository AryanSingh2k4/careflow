# Dr. Emily Rodriguez — Pediatrics

**Fictional demo content:** The clinician, clinic, fees, and processes on this page are fictional portfolio material. This is administrative information only and contains no pediatric assessment or treatment advice.

Dr. Emily Rodriguez is the fictional pediatrics clinician in CareFlow Demo Clinic’s example directory. A pediatric initial consultation is listed at ₹700. A ₹300 follow-up fee applies only when the clinician requested a visit for the same specialty and same concern within 14 calendar days. Otherwise, the initial fee applies. Any requested tests are separate and require their own quote; readers should not infer that a specific lab service is offered.

Appointments depend on the live calendar, including pediatric availability. Reception is open Monday–Saturday from 09:00 to 17:00 IST and closed Sunday and posted holidays. Those administrative hours do not indicate a clinician’s schedule. Please plan to arrive 15 minutes early; arrival more than 10 minutes late may require rebooking. Walk-ins are not assured. Where possible, give 12 hours’ notice when changing an appointment. The fictional policy has no cancellation or missed-appointment fee.

For a child’s visit, the accompanying responsible adult can check the separate paperwork information in this knowledge collection. Do not enter a child’s identifying or health information in a general chat. This page does not establish any real clinician’s identity, qualifications, or availability. For medical questions, use a qualified care professional rather than this portfolio page.
