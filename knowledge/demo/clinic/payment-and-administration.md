# Payment and administrative questions

**Fictional demo content:** This page describes an invented portfolio clinic. It is not a payment instruction for a real healthcare provider, and it contains no real contact or account details.

CareFlow’s portfolio chat does not accept or collect in-app payments. Do not paste card numbers, bank credentials, one-time codes, or other payment secrets into chat. Any payment arrangements for a real appointment must be confirmed through the provider’s own verified administrative channel. This demo does not accept a deposit, store payment details, submit an insurance claim, or state that a bill has been settled.

For reference only, fictional initial fees are ₹600 for general practice, ₹900 for cardiology, and ₹700 for pediatrics. The ₹300 follow-up fee is limited to clinician-requested follow-up in the same specialty for the same concern within 14 calendar days; other visits use the initial fee. Tests, if requested, are separately quoted. The quoted visit fee should not be treated as a quote for tests, medicines, or other services.

Questions about appointment administration can be raised during reception hours: Monday–Saturday, 09:00–17:00 IST, closed Sundays and posted holidays. Reception hours do not imply a slot is available. The demo suggests 12 hours’ notice for appointment changes when possible and imposes no cancellation or no-show fee. Patients should arrive 15 minutes early; a delay of more than 10 minutes may require rebooking. This fictional page cannot verify invoices or a real payment history.
