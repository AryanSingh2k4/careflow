# Patient administrative FAQ

**Fictional demo content:** This FAQ describes an invented CareFlow Demo Clinic for a portfolio. It is not a real provider’s policy, medical advice, or a live support service.

**What are the example consultation fees?** Initial fees are ₹600 for general practice, ₹900 for cardiology, and ₹700 for pediatrics. A ₹300 follow-up applies only if a clinician requested it for the same specialty and same concern within 14 calendar days. Otherwise, the initial fee applies. Tests, if requested and offered, are quoted separately.

**When is reception open?** Monday–Saturday, 09:00–17:00 IST. Sunday and posted holidays are closed. Reception hours do not guarantee a live appointment slot. The booking calendar determines actual availability.

**How early should I arrive?** The example asks visitors to arrive 15 minutes early. If you are more than 10 minutes late, rebooking may be needed. Walk-ins are not guaranteed. If a change is needed, 12 hours’ notice is suggested where possible. The fictional policy has no cancellation or no-show fee.

**Can this chat take payment, check insurance, or retrieve records?** No. It does not process payment, submit claims, access history, or store results. Do not share sensitive details here. Ask a real provider through its verified channel. For test preparation, use instructions from the issuing team; this FAQ gives no fasting or medication directions. The NHS has general blood-test information online, but the issuing team provides test-specific preparation. Clinical questions require a qualified professional.
