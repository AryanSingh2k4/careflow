# Dr. Sarah Chen — General Practice

**Fictional demo content:** CareFlow Demo Clinic, its clinicians, services, and policies described here are invented for a software portfolio. This page is administrative information, not medical advice.

Dr. Sarah Chen is the fictional general practice clinician in this demonstration. The listed initial consultation fee for a general practice appointment is ₹600. A clinician-requested follow-up with the same specialty and same concern within 14 calendar days is ₹300. If either the specialty or concern differs, or the visit falls outside that period, the initial fee applies. Any tests are quoted separately; this page does not promise that a particular test or laboratory service is available.

Appointments are subject to the live booking calendar. Reception is open Monday through Saturday, 09:00–17:00 IST, and closed Sundays and posted holidays; reception hours do not guarantee appointment availability. Patients are asked to arrive 15 minutes before their appointment. Arrival more than 10 minutes late may require rebooking. Walk-ins are not guaranteed. If plans change, 12 hours’ notice is suggested where possible; the demo policy has no cancellation or no-show fee.

This profile does not provide clinical credentials, a schedule, or a way to contact a real person. Use the clinic’s booking and reception information elsewhere in the demo for administrative next steps. Do not share personal health details in a general chat. A real clinic’s arrangements may differ from this fictional example.
