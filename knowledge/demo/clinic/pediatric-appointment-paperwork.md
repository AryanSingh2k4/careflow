# Paperwork for a child’s appointment

**Fictional demo content:** CareFlow Demo Clinic and this pediatric process are invented. This page is administrative only and does not provide advice about a child’s health or consent law.

For a real child’s appointment, the accompanying responsible adult should check with the provider about which forms, identity checks, and permissions are required. Requirements may depend on the provider and circumstances. This portfolio does not determine who may consent, verify guardianship, accept uploaded paperwork, or hold a child’s records. Do not enter a child’s full name, birth date, school, address, medical details, or document image into the general chat. Use the real clinic’s verified secure process for any requested paperwork.

The fictional directory lists Dr. Emily Rodriguez in Pediatrics, with an initial consultation fee of ₹700. A ₹300 follow-up applies only when requested by a clinician for the same specialty and same concern within 14 calendar days; otherwise the initial fee applies. Any tests are separate and may require a separate quote. This demonstration cannot book tests or promise pediatric appointment slots.

Reception in the example is open Monday–Saturday, 09:00–17:00 IST, and closed Sundays and posted holidays. These hours do not guarantee availability. Plan to arrive 15 minutes early; being more than 10 minutes late may require rebooking. If a visit needs changing, 12 hours’ notice is suggested when possible; the demo policy has no cancellation or no-show fee. For clinical questions, contact a qualified care professional directly.
