# Receipts and invoices

**Fictional demo content:** CareFlow Demo Clinic and its billing details are invented for a portfolio. The application has no live payments, invoices, or patient accounts to retrieve.

This knowledge page cannot issue, resend, correct, or verify a receipt. It has no access to a patient’s billing history and should not be used to submit identifying details, transaction references, or payment credentials. For a real visit, ask the provider’s verified administrative team how it supplies receipts and what information it needs. Never share a bank password, card security code, or one-time passcode in a chat.

The example consultation fees are ₹600 for general practice, ₹900 for cardiology, and ₹700 for pediatrics. A ₹300 follow-up fee applies only when a clinician requested the follow-up for the same specialty and same concern within 14 calendar days; otherwise the initial fee applies. Tests, if requested and available, are quoted separately. A consultation fee in this fictional collection is not an invoice or proof of payment, and it does not establish insurance reimbursement.

The demo has no in-app payment feature and no real billing contact details. Reception is open Monday–Saturday 09:00–17:00 IST and closed on Sundays and posted holidays, but those hours do not confirm a live appointment slot. For appointment changes, 12 hours’ notice is suggested where possible; the fictional policy has no cancellation or no-show fee. Arrive 15 minutes early, as delays over 10 minutes may require rebooking.
