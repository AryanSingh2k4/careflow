# Arranging a requested test

**Fictional demo content:** CareFlow Demo Clinic and any workflow described here are fictional. This is administrative guidance only; it does not determine whether a test is needed or appropriate.

If a clinician has requested a test, use the instructions from that clinician or the issuing team to find out where and how it can be arranged. Keep the requisition details available when speaking with the provider. A request does not establish that a service is offered at this clinic, that a slot exists, or that a booking has been made. The portfolio cannot book laboratory services or guarantee a test date. Ask the real provider directly about availability, location, separate charges, and any documents required.

Preparation varies by test. Follow only the test-specific instructions given by the issuing team; this demo does not provide fasting durations or medication guidance. If the directions are unclear, contact that team before the visit. General blood-test information is available from the NHS at [nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/blood-tests](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/blood-tests/), but the issuing team remains the source for specific preparation.

Any test charge is separate from consultation fees and should be quoted separately. The demonstration has no lab booking system, medical record uploads, or results. Do not share requisitions or personal identifiers in a general chat. This page cannot interpret clinical questions or results. Its sole purpose is to point users toward the administrative team responsible for their actual request.
