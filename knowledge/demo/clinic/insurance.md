# Insurance and coverage questions

**Fictional demo content:** CareFlow Demo Clinic is imaginary. This page cannot confirm benefits, eligibility, reimbursement, network status, or claim decisions for any insurer or real clinic.

The portfolio does not connect to an insurer, submit claims, check a policy, or verify whether a service is covered. A listed consultation price is not a promise that an insurer will reimburse it. Coverage can depend on a person’s policy and the provider’s arrangements, neither of which this demo can inspect. Contact the insurer and the real provider using their verified channels before relying on a coverage assumption. Ask what documents they require; do not send policy numbers or sensitive identifiers into general chat.

For context within the fictional example, initial consultation fees are ₹600 for general practice, ₹900 for cardiology, and ₹700 for pediatrics. A ₹300 same-specialty, same-concern follow-up is shown only when requested by a clinician and held within 14 calendar days. Other consultations use the initial fee. Any tests are quoted separately, if offered. These amounts do not include any insurance calculation.

Reception is available Monday through Saturday from 09:00 to 17:00 IST, except Sundays and posted holidays. Opening hours do not guarantee appointment availability. Arrive 15 minutes early; arriving over 10 minutes late may require rebooking. The fictional clinic suggests 12 hours’ notice for changes where possible and has no cancellation or no-show fee. No records or claim history are stored in this demonstration.
