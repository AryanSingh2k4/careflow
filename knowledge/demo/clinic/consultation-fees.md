# Consultation and follow-up fees

**Fictional demo content:** These prices belong only to the invented CareFlow Demo Clinic portfolio scenario. They are not a real provider’s quote or a promise about charges elsewhere.

The initial consultation fees shown in this demo are ₹600 for General Practice with Dr. Sarah Chen, ₹900 for Cardiology with Dr. James Wilson, and ₹700 for Pediatrics with Dr. Emily Rodriguez. Fees are attached to the listed specialty, not a guarantee of a particular appointment time or outcome. If you need a current real-world price, ask the provider directly.

A follow-up costs ₹300 only when a clinician requested it, it is with the same specialty, it concerns the same issue, and it occurs within 14 calendar days. All conditions must be met. If the concern or specialty changes, the visit is outside the 14-day period, or there is no clinician request, the initial fee for that specialty applies. When the visit type is unclear, the demo’s administrative team would need to clarify before booking; this knowledge page does not determine medical urgency or eligibility.

Tests, if requested and available, are separate from consultation fees and should receive a separate quote. The demonstration does not process payment in an app or confirm insurance coverage. No cancellation or no-show fee applies in the fictional policy. Reception is open Monday–Saturday, 09:00–17:00 IST, and closed Sundays and posted holidays; reception hours do not guarantee appointment slots. Plan to arrive 15 minutes early. More than 10 minutes late may mean rebooking.
