# Referrals and specialty appointments

**Fictional demo content:** CareFlow Demo Clinic and all referral workflows described here are invented. This page explains administrative handling only and does not recommend a specialty or assess whether a referral is clinically appropriate.

If a clinician has referred you, keep the referral details available when arranging an appointment. The clinic may need the destination specialty and the issuing clinician’s paperwork to route an administrative request. A referral does not itself reserve a time, guarantee an appointment, or confirm that a particular service is offered. Use the live booking calendar to see available options, or ask reception about the administrative process. Do not send referral documents through a public chat.

The demo’s initial consultation fees are ₹600 for general practice, ₹900 for cardiology, and ₹700 for pediatrics. A ₹300 follow-up applies only when requested by a clinician for the same specialty and same concern within 14 calendar days; otherwise the initial fee applies. Tests are separately quoted, if offered. Insurance coverage and reimbursement are not confirmed by the demo.

Reception hours are Monday–Saturday, 09:00–17:00 IST; Sunday and posted holidays are closed. These are reception hours, not guaranteed appointment slots. Arrive 15 minutes early; more than 10 minutes late may require rebooking. The fictional policy suggests 12 hours’ notice for changes when possible and has no cancellation or no-show fee. No real patient or referral records are held by this portfolio.
