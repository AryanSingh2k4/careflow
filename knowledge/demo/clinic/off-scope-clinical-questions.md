# Clinical questions outside administrative support

**Fictional demo content:** CareFlow Demo Clinic is a portfolio scenario. This page sets an administrative boundary and does not offer medical advice, triage, diagnosis, or treatment.

The knowledge collection can help explain fictional fees, appointment administration, paperwork logistics, and where to direct a request. It cannot decide what a symptom means, recommend a medicine, tell someone whether to wait, determine urgency, or interpret a test. No personal clinical assessment is possible through this portfolio chat. For a clinical concern, contact a qualified healthcare professional using an appropriate care channel. If someone believes they need urgent help, they should use local emergency services rather than rely on this demo or wait for a booking response.

Do not include detailed symptoms, test results, or identifying health information in general chat. For an already requested test, ask the issuing team about logistics and test-specific preparation. This page intentionally provides no symptom examples or clinical decision rules.

Administrative policies in this fictional portfolio show initial visits at ₹600 for general practice, ₹900 for cardiology, and ₹700 for pediatrics. A ₹300 follow-up requires a clinician request, same specialty, same concern, and a visit within 14 calendar days; otherwise the initial fee applies. Tests are separately quoted, if offered. Those fees do not establish what care a person needs. The demo has no real appointments, records, or medical staff.
