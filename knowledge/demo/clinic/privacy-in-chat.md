# Privacy when using clinic chat

**Fictional demo content:** CareFlow Demo Clinic and this chat example are fictional. This page describes safe administrative use of a portfolio interface, not a provider’s privacy notice or legal commitment.

Keep general chat messages free of private health information. Do not enter a patient’s full name, contact details, date of birth, address, policy or record number, payment data, test result, photograph, or document. The portfolio is not connected to a verified patient account, medical record, clinic inbox, or insurer. Sharing information here does not create a clinical relationship or ensure that a care team sees it. Use the real provider’s verified channel and follow its privacy instructions when a secure exchange is needed.

This collection contains invented clinician names, fees, and administrative policies. It includes no real patient case examples. The demo cannot authenticate a user, check consent, manage proxy access, or retrieve a record. For pediatric or guardian paperwork, ask the actual provider which forms and identity checks apply; do not post a child’s identifying details in chat.

Administrative information in the fictional scenario includes reception hours of Monday–Saturday, 09:00–17:00 IST, closed Sunday and posted holidays. Those hours do not promise a slot. The chat does not accept payments, process claims, or guarantee availability. This page is a reminder to keep sensitive information out of an unverified conversational channel, not a substitute for the real organization’s published privacy terms.
