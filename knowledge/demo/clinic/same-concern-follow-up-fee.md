# Same-concern follow-up fee

**Fictional demo content:** CareFlow Demo Clinic, its clinicians, and the fee rules below are invented for a portfolio. This page is administrative only and does not determine whether a return visit is clinically needed.

The fictional fee for an eligible follow-up is ₹300. Eligibility requires all of the following: a clinician requested the follow-up; the visit is with the same specialty; it concerns the same issue; and the appointment takes place within 14 calendar days. The 14-day period is counted in calendar days, not business days. If any condition is missing, the initial consultation fee for the specialty applies. When eligibility is unclear, the actual clinic would need to clarify the booking category; this demo cannot judge the medical content or urgency of a concern.

For comparison, the initial fees in this portfolio are ₹600 for general practice, ₹900 for cardiology, and ₹700 for pediatrics. A change of specialty or a different concern does not qualify for the follow-up price, even if the appointment is within 14 days. A visit outside the period also uses the initial fee. Tests, if requested and offered, are quoted separately from either consultation fee.

These amounts are fictional and are not invoices, payment requests, or insurance coverage estimates. The chat cannot check a real appointment, record, or clinician instruction. Ask a real provider through its verified administrative channel about its own prices and booking classification. No clinical advice is given by this page.
