# Clinic communications and message handling

**Fictional demo content:** All organization details here are invented. This portfolio chat is not a live inbox and does not send messages to real clinicians or reception staff.

Use a real provider’s verified communication channel for appointment-specific questions or changes. A message typed into a portfolio demo does not notify the clinic, become part of a record, or reserve a slot. Avoid sharing personal health details, account credentials, payment information, or document scans in a general chat. The demonstration has no live contact details and cannot promise a response time. Confirm any real instructions directly with the provider that issued them.

Reception hours for the fictional clinic are Monday through Saturday, 09:00–17:00 IST, closed Sundays and posted holidays. These hours describe reception only, not when a clinician is available. Arrival is requested 15 minutes before a booked visit; if more than 10 minutes late, rebooking may be needed. Walk-ins are not guaranteed. Where possible, the demo suggests 12 hours’ notice for changes, with no cancellation or no-show fee.

For clinical questions, contact a qualified professional through an appropriate care channel. The chat cannot assess symptoms or interpret results. The portfolio’s listed clinicians and fees are fictional, and there is no message routing behind their profiles. This page explains that distinction so a visitor does not mistake illustrative content for an active communications service.
