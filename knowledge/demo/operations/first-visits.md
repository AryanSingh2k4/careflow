# A simple first-visit checklist

For a first appointment in the CareFlow Demo Clinic scenario, begin by booking through the live application and reviewing the confirmation for its date, time, and location details if shown. This reference does not create a booking and does not specify a real clinic address or required identity documents. Follow any instructions that the live app actually presents; do not assume a form or feature exists because another clinic uses one.

The demo suggests arriving 15 minutes before the appointment time to allow for ordinary check-in. If you anticipate a delay, contact reception during staffed hours. More than 10 minutes late may mean the appointment is moved or rebooked, subject to availability; it is not guaranteed. Appointment slots come from the live app and can differ from reception hours.

As a general structural reference for how outpatient first visits may be organized, see the NHS [hospital appointments overview](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/hospitals/going-into-hospital/hospital-appointments/). The prose here is original and does not import NHS-specific requirements into this fictional clinic. No medical claims or preparation requirements are being made.

CareFlow's fictional reception schedule is Monday–Saturday, 09:00–17:00 India Standard Time (IST), closed Sundays and posted holidays. Its seed staff are fictional project content. Confirm real requirements directly with a real provider using independently verified information.

**Portfolio disclaimer:** This first-visit checklist and CareFlow Demo Clinic are fictional portfolio content, not a real appointment instruction or medical advice.
