# A deliberately limited directory

CareFlow Demo Clinic uses a small, fictional service directory to make appointment workflows understandable. The three seed specialties are General Practice (Dr. Sarah Chen), Cardiology (Dr. James Wilson), and Pediatrics (Dr. Emily Rodriguez). These are explicitly fictional project seed staff and specialty labels. They do not identify real people, verify credentials, or establish that any real service is offered.

The directory is not a recommendation engine. A specialty label should not be used to route symptoms, judge urgency, or decide what care someone needs. This reference contains no diagnosis, clinical advice, eligibility criteria, or promise about what a visit includes. When choosing an appointment in the demo, use the options presented by the live application and the user's own independently established needs; the app's listing still does not guarantee real-world service.

Availability changes in the live app and is not encoded in this static overview. Reception's fictional staffed hours are Monday through Saturday, 09:00–17:00 India Standard Time, with Sundays and posted holidays closed. Those hours are administrative only. A specialty appearing in the directory does not mean there is an opening during reception hours or on a particular date.

The demo is limited to scheduling concepts. It makes no claims about payment, uploads, chat records, or coverage. Confirm any real service directly with a real provider using independently verified contact details.

**Portfolio disclaimer:** This directory and all named staff are fictional project seed content made for a CareFlow portfolio demo, not real healthcare information or medical advice.
