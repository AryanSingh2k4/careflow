# If an appointment was missed

If you missed a CareFlow Demo Clinic appointment, check the live application for the current booking status and any available next step. This reference cannot mark attendance, restore an appointment, or determine what happened to an individual booking. Contact reception during staffed hours if the app does not make the status clear.

If you know in advance that you cannot attend, cancel or request a change promptly through the app. A target of at least 12 hours' notice is useful where possible, but the fictional demo sets no cancellation fee or no-show fee. If the appointment has already passed, do not assume it has automatically been rebooked or that another time is reserved. Any new appointment depends on slots currently shown in the live app.

Reception is illustratively staffed Monday through Saturday, 09:00–17:00 India Standard Time (IST), and is closed on Sundays and posted holidays. These are administrative hours; appointment availability can differ. No-show handling in this portfolio does not claim a real provider's policy and does not define eligibility, insurance, or clinical consequences.

The seed specialties and named project staff are fictional. This document offers no advice on what care is needed or whether a missed visit can safely be delayed. For an urgent real-world concern, use appropriate local urgent services.

**Portfolio disclaimer:** CareFlow Demo Clinic and the missed-appointment workflow are fictional software portfolio material, not real care, a fee policy, or medical advice.
