# Follow the live booking flow

To make a fictional appointment, use the booking flow in the CareFlow Demo Clinic application. Review the specialty and appointment details offered there, then select from slots the live app actually displays. This knowledge page is not connected to a schedule and cannot reserve, confirm, or create a booking. It lists no exact upcoming dates or availability.

The project seed directory names General Practice with Dr. Sarah Chen, Cardiology with Dr. James Wilson, and Pediatrics with Dr. Emily Rodriguez. All are fictional project seed staff. Specialty names are illustrative labels, not credential claims or guidance for matching symptoms to a clinician. If the app shows no suitable slot, do not assume reception can override its schedule; ask the desk during staffed hours about available administrative options.

The fictional reception window is Monday through Saturday, 09:00–17:00 India Standard Time (IST), excluding Sundays and posted holidays. Booking availability can differ from those hours. Before submitting, check the displayed date, time zone, and appointment details carefully. Retain whatever confirmation the application itself provides and consult that live record if details later appear uncertain.

This portfolio describes no payments, uploads, or chat-record features. A booking shown in a demonstration is not evidence of insurance coverage, a real service, or clinical suitability. For real care, confirm details with a real provider through verified channels.

**Portfolio disclaimer:** CareFlow Demo Clinic and its booking flow are fictional software portfolio content and do not book real care or provide medical advice.
