# Walk-ins depend on capacity

CareFlow Demo Clinic's fictional scenario may accommodate walk-ins only when capacity allows. There is no guarantee that someone arriving without a booking can be seen, and a walk-in request does not reserve a place in a queue or create an appointment. The desk may be unable to accept one if the schedule is full or staff are not available.

Walk-in requests are limited to staffed reception hours: Monday through Saturday, 09:00–17:00 India Standard Time (IST), excluding Sundays and posted holidays. Those are administrative hours, not a promise of clinical coverage. Appointment slots are shown only in the live app and may differ from the reception window. Checking the app first can show bookable appointments, but it cannot guarantee acceptance of an unscheduled arrival.

If the visit needs a specific time, use the app's live booking flow and rely on the confirmation it displays. The fictional directory names General Practice with Dr. Sarah Chen, Cardiology with Dr. James Wilson, and Pediatrics with Dr. Emily Rodriguez; all are project seed staff, not verified real clinicians. The list should not be used for symptom routing or urgency decisions.

This portfolio describes no payment, upload, or chat-record features and makes no coverage or real-service guarantee. A walk-in policy cannot advise whether a concern can wait; use appropriate local services for urgent real-world needs.

**Portfolio disclaimer:** Walk-in handling, CareFlow Demo Clinic, and its seed staff are fictional portfolio content, not real clinic operations or medical advice.
