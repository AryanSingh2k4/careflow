# Choose a new time through the app

For an existing CareFlow Demo Clinic appointment, use the live app's reschedule option if it is available. Review the newly displayed date and time before confirming, then check the resulting appointment details. This static document cannot change a booking. If no reschedule control appears or the result is uncertain, contact reception during its staffed window for administrative guidance.

Where possible, make the request at least 12 hours before the current appointment. That is a planning target, not a fee trigger. The fictional demo specifies no cancellation or no-show fees. A new time is subject to live availability; the app may not offer a like-for-like replacement, and reception cannot promise a slot that is not actually available. Do not treat a request as complete until the app presents an updated confirmation.

Reception is illustrated as open Monday through Saturday, 09:00–17:00 India Standard Time (IST), excluding Sundays and posted holidays. Desk hours are not appointment availability. Check the live app for current slots, which can differ from reception hours, and check the time zone in the appointment details if planning across regions.
