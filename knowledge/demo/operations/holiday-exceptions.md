# Checking around a closure

In this fictional example, CareFlow Demo Clinic reception is closed every Sunday and on holidays that the clinic has posted as closure days. A public holiday should not be assumed to have a particular status unless the fictional clinic has posted it; this reference contains no calendar of upcoming holiday dates. Likewise, a day not mentioned here should not be treated as a confirmed open day.

The usual reception window on staffed days is Monday through Saturday, 09:00–17:00 India Standard Time (IST). Holiday exceptions can alter that administrative schedule. Appointment availability is separate: the live app alone shows current bookable slots, and those may differ from desk hours or from a closure notice. Do not infer a slot from a routine weekly schedule.

If an appointment appears near a closure, rely on the appointment details shown by the live application and seek clarification during staffed reception hours when needed. Any request to change or cancel should be made promptly, with a target of at least 12 hours in advance where possible. No cancellation or no-show fee is specified for this fictional portfolio. A closure does not create a promise that another date or time can be substituted.

This document is operational information for a demo only. It does not define emergency coverage or an after-hours clinical pathway. Use appropriate local urgent services for urgent real-world needs rather than relying on a fictional closure notice.

**Portfolio disclaimer:** CareFlow Demo Clinic and this holiday-handling description are fictional portfolio material, not a real clinic calendar or medical guidance.
