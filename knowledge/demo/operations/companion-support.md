# Check arrangements in advance

Someone planning to bring a companion to a CareFlow Demo Clinic appointment should ask fictional reception about the visit's practical arrangements before arriving. The portfolio does not set a guest limit, guarantee that companions can attend, or specify room capacity or privacy rules. Reception can discuss a request, but only verified information from a real provider can establish real attendance rules.

The app's live booking flow is for appointment slots and does not imply that a companion has been added or approved. Check the appointment details in the app, then use reception during staffed hours for administrative questions. The fictional desk is open Monday through Saturday, 09:00–17:00 India Standard Time (IST), and closed Sundays and posted holidays. Slot availability can differ from this window.

If the appointment is for a child or someone who needs assistance, this page still does not define legal authority, consent, or required documentation. Those questions depend on real-world context and should be checked with the actual provider. The demo seed staff are fictional project content and do not imply a particular support arrangement.

No diagnosis, triage, or coverage information is offered here. The portfolio also does not specify payments, uploads, or chat-record features. Avoid treating a request as confirmed until a responsible real provider has confirmed it for a real appointment.

**Portfolio disclaimer:** CareFlow Demo Clinic's companion guidance and every staff name are fictional portfolio material, not a real attendance policy or medical advice.
