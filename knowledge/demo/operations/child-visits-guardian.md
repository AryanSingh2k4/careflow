# Keep booking details clear

In the CareFlow Demo Clinic scenario, a parent or guardian arranging a child's appointment should use the live app's available booking flow and follow the app's own prompts about whose appointment is being booked. Check the displayed details before confirming. This document does not define consent law, custody authority, identity checks, or required documents; requirements vary in real settings and must be verified with the actual provider.

The demo seed directory includes Pediatrics with Dr. Emily Rodriguez, alongside General Practice (Dr. Sarah Chen) and Cardiology (Dr. James Wilson). All three are fictional project seed staff. These labels do not verify credentials, imply a real pediatric service, or tell a guardian which specialty is suitable for a child. This is not symptom-routing guidance.

Reception is illustratively staffed Monday–Saturday, 09:00–17:00 India Standard Time (IST), with Sunday and posted holidays closed. Those are administrative hours, not appointment availability; current slots appear only in the live application and can differ. The demo suggests arriving 15 minutes early. If more than 10 minutes late, staff may move or rebook the visit subject to availability.

For a real child's visit, confirm guardian and preparation requirements directly with a real provider using verified contact information. This portfolio does not describe uploaded documents, payment, or chat records and cannot advise on care.
