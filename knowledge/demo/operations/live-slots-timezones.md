# Treat displayed slots as current, not this article

Appointment availability belongs to the live CareFlow application. This static reference has no exact upcoming date, time, or opening count, and cannot tell whether a slot remains available. Check the live app immediately before booking; an option can change while someone is reviewing it. If a slot is no longer displayed, treat it as unavailable and choose from the options currently offered.

The fictional reception schedule is Monday through Saturday, 09:00–17:00 India Standard Time (IST), with Sundays and posted holidays closed. These are reception hours only. Appointment slots can differ from them, so an open desk does not prove a clinician has an appointment opening, and an app slot should be read from its own displayed details rather than inferred from the weekly desk schedule.

When booking across locations or devices, inspect the date and time zone shown on the confirmation. This portfolio uses IST as the reference for reception hours, but the app's presentation should be checked for each appointment. If the displayed time seems inconsistent, avoid relying on a mental conversion; revisit the live booking details and ask reception during staffed hours to clarify the administrative record.

No availability listed here should be treated as a reservation. The document does not promise that a specific specialty or staff member is bookable at a certain time. It also does not provide clinical advice or guarantee a real service.
