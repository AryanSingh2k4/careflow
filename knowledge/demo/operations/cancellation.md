# If plans change

If you no longer expect to attend a CareFlow Demo Clinic appointment, use the live application's cancellation controls and check for its confirmation. A static knowledge page cannot cancel an appointment or update a booking record. If the control is unavailable or the result is unclear, contact fictional reception during its staffed hours so the administrative next step can be discussed.

Please make changes promptly. Where possible, the demo's planning target is to cancel at least 12 hours before the appointment. This is a courtesy target, not a stated penalty threshold. The fictional portfolio specifies no cancellation fee and no no-show fee. A late request may still be worth making; the app or reception can indicate what options are actually available, without guaranteeing that a replacement appointment can be arranged.

Reception is illustrated as open Monday through Saturday, 09:00–17:00 India Standard Time (IST), and closed Sundays and posted holidays. These are not appointment hours; live app availability can differ. Do not infer a new opening from a cancellation or assume that a released time will remain visible.

This document addresses scheduling administration only. It does not describe payment handling, chat records, or clinical services, and does not advise whether a health concern can wait. For urgent real-world concerns, use appropriate local services rather than a fictional booking policy.

**Portfolio disclaimer:** CareFlow Demo Clinic and its cancellation process are fictional portfolio material, not a real appointment service, fee schedule, or medical advice.
