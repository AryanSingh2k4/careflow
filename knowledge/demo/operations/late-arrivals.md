# Tell reception as soon as practical

If you expect to reach CareFlow Demo Clinic after your appointment time, contact the fictional reception desk as soon as practical during staffed hours. The desk can explain the available administrative next step, but a message or call does not hold the appointment or guarantee that it can proceed. The live app remains the source for any updated booking details.

The demo suggests arriving 15 minutes before the scheduled time for check-in. If someone is more than 10 minutes late, the clinic may move or rebook the appointment, subject to availability. This is a possibility rather than an automatic outcome or guaranteed grace period. Staff may consider what the live schedule allows; this page does not promise that a late arrival will be seen or that a replacement slot exists.

Reception hours are Monday through Saturday, 09:00–17:00 India Standard Time (IST); Sundays and posted holidays are closed. These are desk hours, not appointment availability. Check the live app for slots and any current booking information. The fictional portfolio specifies no late-arrival, cancellation, or no-show fee.

This is an administrative note only. It cannot assess symptoms or determine whether waiting is appropriate. If a real-world concern is urgent, seek appropriate local care instead of relying on a fictional scheduling process.

**Portfolio disclaimer:** CareFlow Demo Clinic, its arrival guidance, staff, and schedule are fictional portfolio content and do not provide real services or medical advice.
