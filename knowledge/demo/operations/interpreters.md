# Ask before the appointment

If language support may be needed for a CareFlow Demo Clinic visit, contact fictional reception during its staffed hours and ask what arrangements, if any, could be discussed. This portfolio does not promise that an interpreter is available, identify supported languages, or describe a booking service. The app is for scheduling in this demo and no chat-record or interpreter-request feature is specified.

Reception is illustratively staffed Monday through Saturday, 09:00–17:00 India Standard Time (IST), excluding Sundays and posted holidays. Appointment availability appears only in the live app and may not match the desk's hours. A booking does not itself confirm language support. If reception cannot confirm an arrangement, do not assume one has been made; seek verified information from the relevant real provider for an actual visit.

The fictional seed staff are Dr. Sarah Chen (General Practice), Dr. James Wilson (Cardiology), and Dr. Emily Rodriguez (Pediatrics). These are project seed names, not real providers, credential claims, or indicators of which language a clinician speaks. Specialty labels should not be used to route symptoms or judge care needs.

This page contains no translation of clinical information and no medical advice. It also makes no promise about coverage or real service. For real-world language access, verify options directly with the provider or service involved.

**Portfolio disclaimer:** Language support, staff, and workflows mentioned for CareFlow Demo Clinic are fictional portfolio material, not a real interpreter offer or medical advice.
