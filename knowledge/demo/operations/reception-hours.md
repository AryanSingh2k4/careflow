# When the fictional desk is staffed

Reception at CareFlow Demo Clinic is illustrated as staffed Monday through Saturday from 09:00 to 17:00 India Standard Time (IST). The desk is closed on Sundays and on posted holidays. These hours describe when reception is available for routine administrative help in the fictional scenario. They are not clinical coverage hours, an emergency contact schedule, or a promise that a clinician is available at every moment.

Appointment slots are displayed only in the live application, and availability can differ from reception hours. A desk being open does not mean a same-day or particular-specialty appointment can be booked. This page deliberately does not list specific upcoming dates or open slots; check the app for current options. If the app does not show a suitable option, contact reception during staffed hours to ask about the next administrative step, without assuming a slot can be added.

For planning, allow time to complete check-in before the displayed appointment time. If you expect to arrive after your slot, reception may need to discuss moving or rebooking it, subject to availability. Outside staffed hours, use the live app for scheduling where available and wait for the desk to reopen for routine questions. The fictional clinic does not define an after-hours clinical service here.

**Portfolio disclaimer:** This schedule belongs only to the fictional CareFlow Demo Clinic portfolio scenario. It is not a real clinic timetable, care guarantee, or medical advice.
